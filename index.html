Flagship | Live product

Review Kiosk

Problem
Small businesses struggle to collect Google reviews and to hear unhappy customers privately.
Solution built
A QR code the business keeps on its desk. Customers scan it and respond in any language, by voice or text, translated to English. Happy customers get a one-tap Google review in their own words or an AI-polished version. Unhappy customers send feedback privately to the owner.
Outcome
A live product with Free and Pro plans, monthly AI usage limits to control cost, and subscription payments tested end to end in Stripe test mode.
  • AI-written reviews
  • QR workflow
  • SaaS
  • Voice reviews
  • Vercel
  • Supabase
  • Stripe

AI | Prototype

SecureKnowAI

Problem
Banks, hospitals and airlines cannot upload confidential documents to cloud AI tools.
Solution built
A fully local, on-device document Q&A using Gemma 3 via Ollama. No data leaves the machine. It answers questions across PDF, Excel and CSV files, has a summarisation mode and a web interface for uploading and removing documents.
Outcome
Validated against a self-built test set of 16 business questions, including cross-document questions.
  • Gemma 3
  • Ollama
  • Private LLM
  • Document Q&A
